## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Turn every walk into a wildlife hunt.

PakimonGO is a real-world nature discovery app: spot an animal, snap a
photo, and let our AI identify what you found. Every genuine wild
sighting earns you points — capture more species, build your personal
collection, and climb the leaderboard.

**How it works**
- Open the camera, photograph an animal you actually see.
- Our AI (not a human reviewer) identifies the species and checks the
  context — wild sightings score highest, so there's no reward for
  photographing pets or zoo animals as if they were wild.
- Build a personal collection of every species you've captured, complete
  with your best photo, capture count, and points per species.

**A living map, privacy-first**
See where the community has been spotting wildlife on a real map —
locations are always shown as an approximate area, never your exact
GPS position, so you can share a sighting without sharing where you live.

**Connect with other explorers**
Follow other wildlife spotters, react to their captures, comment, and
post 24-hour stories from the field. Join group quests with a squad and
work toward shared community goals.

**Built responsibly**
- A 13+ age gate applies to every new account.
- Report and block tools are built in, with an audit trail behind the
  scenes — this isn't a UI-only promise.
- We tell you plainly what scoring can and can't detect; see our
  in-app onboarding for how scoring honesty works.
- Read our full privacy policy for exactly what we collect and why:
  `[TODO: privacy policy URL]`.

Whether you're a casual dog-walker who wants to notice more, or someone
who already tracks every bird in the yard, PakimonGO turns real
observation into a game worth coming back to.
